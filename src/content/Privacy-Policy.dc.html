Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

South Bay STEM Association ("SBSA," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through southbaystem.org, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and phone number when you volunteer, register for a program, contact us, or donate. We may also collect limited technical information automatically, such as browser type, device type, and pages visited, through standard web analytics.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to operate and improve our programs, respond to inquiries, process donations, coordinate volunteers, and send occasional updates about SBSA. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar tools to understand how visitors use the site and to improve functionality. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; some site features may not work as intended if you do.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our website, process donations, or communicate with volunteers and supporters, and who are obligated to protect it. We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Children's Privacy

Our programs serve K–12 students, but we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children online without parent or guardian involvement. Program registration and communication are handled through parents, guardians, or partner schools.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

You may ask us to update, correct, or delete your personal information, or unsubscribe from our communications at any time, by contacting us using the information below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@southbaystem.org.
