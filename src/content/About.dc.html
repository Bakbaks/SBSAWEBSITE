We want every student to have real access to hands-on STEM learning. We're getting there with free, volunteer-led workshops in schools across the South Bay.

Our History

Where we started

2023 · Founded

SBSA is founded as a student- and volunteer-led nonprofit.

2024 · 6/17

First Zero Robotics Summer Program, 1 team, 11 members.

2025 · 3/16

Awarded the Hershey Heartwarming Grant.

2025 · 5/9

It's official, approved to host workshops with TUSD. First workshop at Arlington Elementary, a 4-week program.

2025 · 6/11

Second Zero Robotics Summer Program, 2 teams, 16 members.

2025 · 9/16

Workshops reaching more schools. 18 workshops total at Arlington, Lincoln, and Wood Elementary.

2026 · 2/2

18 workshops during the spring season at Arlington, Lincoln, and Wood Elementary.

2026 · 6/11

Third Zero Robotics Summer Program, 4 teams, 30 members.

What's Next

We're working to bring our STEM Workshops and Zero Robotics programs to more schools across the South Bay, and to recruit more student and volunteer mentors to help us get there.
