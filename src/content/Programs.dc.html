STEM Workshops

In-person, hands-on science and engineering

Our STEM Workshops bring hands-on physical science and engineering projects directly into the classroom during the school year, hosted in partnership with local elementary schools — currently in Torrance, in partnership with Torrance Unified School District.

  • Beginner-friendly, project-based lessons led by student and volunteer mentors
  • Hosted in partnership with local elementary schools
  • ~30 workshops run so far, reaching students across 3 schools

Zero Robotics

An online coding competition for middle schoolers

Zero Robotics is an online coding competition hosted by MIT, held over the summer. Middle schoolers learn to program, strategize, and work as a team to produce code that runs on the International Space Station.

  • What began as a six-student online coding program has grown each year
  • Runs online over the summer, open to middle school students
  • Teaches programming, strategy, and teamwork through friendly competition
